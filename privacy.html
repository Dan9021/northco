Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information Northco collects, how we use it and the choices you have. Northco is a virtual store within Roblox, and this website is its home on the web.

Information on this website

This website has no order forms or advertising trackers, and you can read every page without giving us any personal details. To load, the pages fetch fonts from Google Fonts and code from jsDelivr, and those services receive your IP address when they do. Our hosting platform may record standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open, to keep the site running and secure.

Signing in with Discord

The Profile page lets you sign in with your Discord account. This is optional. When you do, Discord shares your Discord user ID, username, avatar and email address with us. We keep a profile for you with our database provider, Supabase: your display name, avatar, the choices you make on the Profile page, and whether you are an admin or have been banned. Supabase also stores your sign-in details, including your email address, to run the login. Your email address and Discord ID are shown only to you, on your own Profile page, and only if you turn those options on. Admins can see profile names and account status. Your browser keeps a sign-in session in its local storage so that you stay signed in, and you can end it at any time with Log out.

Information in Roblox

When you visit Northco in Roblox, the platform shares limited information with us, such as your username, display name and user ID. We also see what you do in our experience, for example chat messages and in-game actions. Roblox collects and handles this information under its own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control.

Community and staff applications

If you join our Discord server or apply for a staff role, you may give us your Discord name, your Roblox username and the answers in your application. We use this only to run the community and review applications. If you apply on the Employment page, we store your application (your Discord name, Roblox username, time zone and your answers) in our database with Supabase. Only you and our admins can see it, admins may add a private note about their decision, and you can withdraw a pending application at any time. Discord handles its own data under its own policies.

How we use information

  • To run and improve the Northco experience.
  • To keep players safe and enforce our rules.
  • To review staff applications and manage roles.
  • To answer your questions.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the platforms we run on (Roblox and Discord), with Supabase, which stores sign-in and profile data for us, with moderators who need it to enforce our rules, and when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep application details while they are needed to run the team, and delete them once they are no longer needed. We keep your profile until you ask us to delete it. Roblox and Discord keep their own records under their own policies.

Children

Roblox is used by people of many ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what the platforms share with us. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it (including your profile), or leave our Discord server at any time. Contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Northco grows. The date at the top shows the latest version.
